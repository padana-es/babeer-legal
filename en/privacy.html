Privacy Policy

BaBeer is an app for discovering beers, logging what you try, and competing in leagues with your friends. This page explains what data we process, why, who with, and how to exercise your rights.

In short

  • We don't show ads and we don't sell your data.
  • We don't use third-party analytics tools or trackers.
  • Your email is private: nobody else sees it.
  • We store the exact location of each check-in, but we never show it exactly to other people.
  • You can delete your account from the app whenever you want.

3. What other people see

  • Any user: your @username and your profile photo or avatar.
  • Your friends: your full profile (display name, level, XP, badges, stats, and posts) and your map, where your check-ins appear at most grouped by town or at the venue's public coordinates, never at your exact position.
  • Members of your leagues: your @username, your avatar, your points, and the posts of the check-ins that count in that league.
  • Nobody but you: your email, the exact coordinates of your check-ins, and the position of your personal places (which have no coordinates).
  • Reports are anonymous: the reported person doesn't know who reported them. Your VAR votes are anonymous.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and your content: for as long as you have the account.
  • If you delete your account: it disappears immediately for everyone else and you leave your active leagues. You have 30 days to change your mind. After that, we delete your personal data and your photos and remove your sign-in identity. Your league history (including your Cheers, which give points) is kept anonymized, as "Deleted account," so other players' standings don't break. The exact location of those check-ins is reduced to an approximate area (about 1 km). More details in Delete your account.
  • Bell alerts: 90 days.
  • Push notification delivery log: 35 days.
  • Push tokens: until you sign out on that phone, the notification service reports it is no longer valid, or you delete your account.
  • Reports and moderation decisions: kept as evidence for as long as needed for moderation and to handle possible claims.
  • Feedback: until it is resolved and, after that, while it helps us improve the app; at most, until your account is deleted.
  • Data requests: for as long as needed to prove that we handled them.

8. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability, and withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the processing carried out before.

  • Access: from the app, in Settings › Privacy › Request my data. We will send a copy of your data to your Google email.
  • Rectification: you can change your photo, your bio, and other profile data in the app; for anything else, write to us.
  • Erasure: from the app (how to delete your account) or by email.
  • Any right: write to padana.es@gmail.com from your account's Google email, including your @username, so we can verify it's you.

We answer within one month at most. If the request is complex, this may be extended by two more months, and we will tell you within the first month, explaining why.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es). We'd appreciate it if you wrote to us first so we can try to resolve it.

9. Adults only

BaBeer is only for people of legal drinking age in their country: 18 or older (21 in the US). It is not intended for minors. You confirm your age when you create your account. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we delete it. If you suspect so, let us know at the contact email.

10. Security

  • All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • The database enforces row-by-row access control: each person can only read what they are allowed to.
  • Check-in photos are kept in private storage and shown through temporary links to the people who can see them.
  • Your profile photo is served through a public link, because any user can see it.
  • The app contains no secret keys.

11. No advertising or analytics

BaBeer doesn't show ads, doesn't sell or disclose your data, and doesn't include third-party analytics or advertising tools. Firebase Cloud Messaging is used only to deliver push notifications.

12. These web pages

This site is static: it uses no cookies, no JavaScript, and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security reasons, under its own privacy policy.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with its date. If the change is significant, the app will show it to you and ask you to accept it again.

14. Applicable law

This policy is governed by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights, and other Spanish law. The supervisory authority is the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
