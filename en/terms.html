Terms of Use

These terms govern the use of the BaBeer app. By creating your account you accept them, together with the Privacy Policy. BaBeer is an independent project developed by a team of three people (the owner's details are in the Legal notice).

1. Who can use BaBeer

  • Only people of legal drinking age in their country: 18 or older (21 in the US). You confirm this when you create your account, and it must be true.
  • You need a Google account to sign in.
  • If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we delete it.

2. Your account

  • Your @username is public and can't be changed after you create your account.
  • You are responsible for what is done with your account. If you think someone is using it without your permission, let us know.
  • You can delete your account whenever you want (see section 11).

3. Community rules

BaBeer is for discovering beers and competing in good spirits. You may not post or send:

  • sexual content or nudity;
  • violence, threats, or content that promotes them;
  • hate, harassment, or insults against people or groups;
  • illegal content or content that infringes the rights of others (for example, photos of other people without their permission);
  • spam, advertising, or misleading links;
  • impersonation of another person or brand;
  • content that encourages drinking to excess, drinking fast, or drinking as a quantity contest, that shows or encourages minors drinking, or that links drinking with driving;
  • other people's private data (addresses, phone numbers, documents…).

You also may not cheat in the competition (for example, by faking your location or logging beers you aren't having), use the app in an automated way, try to access other people's data, or attack or overload the service.

4. Reports, blocking, and moderation

  • From the app you can report posts, comments, users, and profile photos. Reports are anonymous to the reported person.
  • We review reports and respond within 48 hours at most.
  • If content breaks these rules, we may hide it (it stops being visible to everyone; its author sees that it was removed for breaking the terms), remove a profile photo, or suspend the account, temporarily or permanently, depending on how serious it is or whether it is repeated.
  • You can block another user from their profile: you stop seeing each other's profiles, posts, and comments, the friendship ends, and you can't send each other requests, tag each other, or get notifications from each other. The competition doesn't change: if you share a league, you both stay in it. Blocking is silent and you can undo it in Settings.
  • If you think a moderation decision is a mistake, write to us at padana.es@gmail.com and we will review it.

5. The competition

  • The competition rules (points, leagues, VAR, badges, and XP) are explained inside the app and may change between seasons. A league's rules are fixed once that league starts.
  • VAR is a review among the league's members: its decisions apply inside the app.
  • Points, XP, badges, and prizes exist only inside BaBeer: they have no monetary value and can't be redeemed.

6. Responsible drinking

  • BaBeer rewards discovering beers, not drinking more.
  • 0.0 beers count just the same.
  • Never drink if you're going to drive.
  • Adults only.
  • BaBeer makes no health claims. If you drink, do so in moderation and at your own responsibility.
