Delete your BaBeer account

This page explains how to delete your account in the BaBeer app and its associated data, with or without the app.

From the app

  1. Open BaBeer and go to Profile › Settings (the gear at the top right).
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  3. Read what is deleted and what is kept, type your @username, and tap the red Delete my account button.

Your account disappears immediately for everyone else, you are signed out, and you leave your active leagues at that moment.

You have 30 days to change your mind

  • For 30 days you can recover the account: sign in again with your Google account and tap Recover my account.
  • When you recover it, everything comes back except leagues: the active leagues you left are not restored.
  • After 30 days, the deletion is final and can't be undone.

Without the app, by email

If you can't use the app, write to padana.es@gmail.com:

  • from your account's Google email (so we can verify the account is yours);
  • with the subject "Delete my BaBeer account";
  • including your @username.

We will reply and complete the deletion within one month at most.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: @username, display name, bio, and profile photo.
  • Your photos and your posts.
  • Your comments.
  • Your friendships and friend requests.
  • Your personal places.
  • Your notifications, your push tokens, and your preferences.
  • Your feedback (problems and ideas).
  • Your sign-in identity (the link to your Google account and your email).

What is kept anonymized, and why

Leagues are a competition between several people. So that other players' standings and history don't break, the following is kept without any data that identifies you, under the name "Deleted account":

  • your league history, your points, and your XP;
  • your Cheers, which give points in leagues;
  • your check-ins as a competitive record, without photos;
  • your VAR votes, which were already anonymous.

The exact location of those check-ins is reduced to an approximate area (about 1 km) at final deletion.

Reports and moderation decisions are kept as evidence for as long as needed (see the Privacy Policy).

Timing

  • From the app: a 30-day grace period, then final deletion.
  • By email: one month at most.
